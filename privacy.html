Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

HomeSuite is a private app used only by the family that runs it, on their own computer at home.

Google data

  • HomeSuite reads the Google Calendar events of family members who connect their account, to show them in the family app.
  • It creates or deletes calendar events only when a family member asks it to in the app.
  • Calendar data stays on the family's own computer. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The sign-in permission is stored encrypted on that computer. Anyone who connected can remove it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other data

Everything else the family enters (lists, recipes, notes, photos) stays on their own computer and its private backups.
