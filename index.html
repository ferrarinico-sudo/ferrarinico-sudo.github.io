HomeSuite

A private family app, not a public service.

HomeSuite is a small set of personal apps that one family runs on its own home computer. They keep the family's shared lists, recipes, plans and calendar view in one place.

What it does with Google

With each family member's permission, HomeSuite reads their Google Calendar to show upcoming events in the family app, and adds events they create there (for example dates from a school letter) to their calendar. It does nothing else with Google data.

Who can use it

Only members of the family who run it. There are no sign-ups and no other users.
